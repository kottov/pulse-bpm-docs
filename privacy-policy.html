3. Microphone & audio — please read this carefully

The app uses your device microphone in two clearly different ways:

3.1 On-device tempo (BPM) detection

The core feature of Live BPM listens to nearby sound through your microphone to estimate the tempo (beats per minute) in real time. This audio analysis runs entirely on your device. We do not record it, do not save it, and do not transmit the raw audio to us or to any third party. Once tempo has been estimated, the underlying sound is discarded from memory.

3.2 “Track ID” song recognition (optional)

If you use the Track ID feature, the app identifies the music that is playing using Apple’s Shazam music-recognition service. On iOS this uses Apple’s built-in ShazamKit; on Android it uses an equivalent Shazam-based recognizer that obtains a short-lived access token from our backend (Supabase). In both cases, your device converts the captured sound into a numerical acoustic “fingerprint” and sends that fingerprint — not a recording — to the Shazam service to look for a match. As part of this request, the service may receive technical information such as your IP address and a randomly generated identifier. Apple’s handling of this data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Track ID is a paid feature (and remains available to certain users carried over from our previous app, retape). It runs when you start it — or, if you turn on the optional “auto-start Track ID” setting, automatically each time a microphone detection begins, until you turn that setting off.

When a song is identified, the app may show streaming links for it. To build those links it sends the matched song’s Apple Music URL (and your store country) to the Odesli / Song.link service, which returns equivalent links for other platforms. No microphone audio is sent to Odesli.

5. Accounts & sign-in

Most of the app works without an account. If you want to sync your data across devices, you can create an account using:

  • Sign in with Google — we receive your email, display name and profile picture URL;
  • Sign in with Apple — we receive your name (if you share it) and either your real email or an Apple private relay address;
  • Email one-time code — we receive your email address and verify it with a code.

Authentication and account storage are provided by Supabase (see Section 9). When you are signed in, your email and display name are also shared with our subscription provider (RevenueCat) to manage your entitlements. For Sign in with Apple, our backend may store an Apple-issued refresh token so that, if you later delete your account, we can ask Apple to revoke the app’s access on your behalf.

6. Cloud sync & your library

If you are signed in, certain data is stored on our backend (Supabase) so it can be synced across your devices:

  • App settings sync for all signed-in users, so you can restore them after reinstalling;
  • Your session library (sessions, names, collections and tool results) syncs only for users with an active paid subscription. Free users and users who made a one-time purchase keep their sessions on the device only — they are not uploaded.

If you are not signed in, your data stays on your device.

7. Analytics, crash & performance data

We use Google Firebase tools to understand and improve the app:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — aggregated usage and feature analytics. Analytics events do not include your name or email.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reporting (released app versions only).
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — app and network performance metrics (released app versions only).
  • Firebase Remote Config — to configure features and roll out changes safely.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, advertising and analytics that rely on device identifiers are managed through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tool and Google Consent Mode; until you grant consent, those identifiers are not used for advertising/analytics purposes for you. Crash reporting and performance monitoring run in released versions to keep the app stable and secure on the basis of our legitimate interest; you can object to this by contacting us. We exclude analytics, crash and performance data generated by our own development and test builds from our production reporting where technically feasible.

8. Advertising (free version only)

The free version of the app shows ads through Google AdMob. The paid tiers are ad-free. To show ads, AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising identifier and ad-request data, and may use this for personalised advertising depending on your choices.

Your controls:

  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • In the EEA/UK, your advertising/analytics consent is collected through the consent prompt described in Section 7. You can also withdraw or change your choices at any time using the device ad settings above, or by emailing us.
  • You can also email us at kottov@gmail.com to ask us to limit the processing of your data for advertising.

For more on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see Google’s “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”.

11. How long we keep data

  • Microphone audio for tempo detection is processed in real time and never stored.
  • Account, settings and synced library data are kept while your account is active and deleted (from the cloud) when you delete your account, subject to short technical backup windows held by our providers.
  • Cloud copies of items you delete. When you delete an individual synced session, it is removed from your devices; a soft-deletion marker may remain on our backend until you delete your account.
  • Local data on your device stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics, crash and performance data are retained according to the relevant Firebase product’s retention settings and Google’s defaults.
  • Purchase records are retained as needed to manage entitlements and meet legal/accounting obligations.

14. Deleting your account & data

You can delete your account from within the app (in the account settings). When you do:

  • your account and the data synced to our backend (your synced settings and, for subscribers, your synced library) are deleted from the cloud;
  • we make a best-effort request to delete your subscriber record at our subscription provider, and — for Sign in with Apple — to revoke the app’s access with Apple;
  • data stored locally on your device is not automatically erased by account deletion. To remove local data, uninstall the app (which deletes all on-device data) or, where the app provides it, delete individual items in the app.

You can also request deletion by emailing kottov@gmail.com, or via our web deletion page at kottov.github.io/pulse-bpm-docs/account-deletion.html. Note that cancelling a subscription is handled through the App Store or Google Play and is separate from deleting your account.

17. Migration from the previous app (retape)

Live BPM is the successor to our earlier app, retape. If you previously used retape on the same device, the app migrates your existing local data (such as settings and, for previous Plus users, saved sessions) into Live BPM once, on your device, on first launch. Like any other data in the app, migrated data may later sync to the cloud if you sign in — your settings for all signed-in users, and your session library for subscribers (see Sections 6 and 9).
