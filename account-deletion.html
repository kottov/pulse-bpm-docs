Delete Your Live BPM Account & Data

App: Live BPM (App Store: “Live BPM: Tap Tempo & Counter”) · Developer: Kostiantyn Pogorielov
Last updated: 25 June 2026

This page explains how to delete your Live BPM account and the data associated with it. It applies to the Live BPM app for iOS and Android.

Option 1 — delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Live BPM.
  2. Go to your Account settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your account and the data synced to our backend.

Option 2 — request by email

If you can no longer access the app (for example, you have uninstalled it), email kottov@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line “Delete my account”. We will verify your request and process the deletion. We aim to respond within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record and the data synced to our backend — your synced app settings and, for users with an active paid subscription, your synced session library (sessions, names, collections, and tool results).
  • We make a best-effort request to delete your subscriber record at our subscription provider and, for Sign in with Apple, to revoke the app’s access with Apple.

What is not deleted automatically

  • Data stored locally on your device is not erased by account deletion. To remove it, uninstall the app (which deletes all on-device data) or, where the app provides it, delete individual items in the app.
  • Your subscription is billed by the App Store or Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • We may retain limited records where required for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes, and our service providers may keep data in short-term technical backups for a limited time before it expires.
For full details on what data we hold and how we handle it, see our Privacy Policy.
